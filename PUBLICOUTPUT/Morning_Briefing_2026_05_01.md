Unexpected weather events or a sudden demand spike could tighten storage balances.

### 4. Production and supply trend
Production and supply trends are loosening as Northeast output declines and some producers hold output flat in response to weak pricing.

Headlines show that Northeast production is down, with some operators maintaining flat output strategies amid weak basin pricing. This supply-side softness is contributing to lower spot prices in the region and easing overall market tightness. The trend is being watched for potential reversal if demand or pricing incentives change.

A rapid recovery in production or a shift in producer strategy could reverse the loosening trend.

### 5. Power burn and demand pull
Power burn and demand pull are tightening, with mixed PJM power prices and growing data center demand supporting gas usage in key regions.

PJM's mixed peak price moves and ongoing data center growth in Texas and the MidAtlantic are supporting gas demand for power generation. These factors are offsetting some of the bearish signals from storage and production, keeping power burn in focus as a key driver. Weather and regulatory developments will continue to influence the strength and direction of this demand pull.

A significant drop in power sector demand or a surge in renewable generation could weaken this narrative.
