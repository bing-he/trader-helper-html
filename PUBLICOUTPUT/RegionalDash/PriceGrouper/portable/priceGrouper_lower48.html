How PriceGrouper Works

PriceGrouper is a relative-value screen for basis markets. It groups components by how their basis moves together versus Henry Hub, using roughly the last two years of daily basis-correlation history and evaluating each season separately. That means the groups reflect seasonal trading behavior, not just one all-year average relationship.

Season definitions are Winter = Dec-Feb, Spring = Mar-May, Summer = Jun-Aug, Autumn = Sep-Nov. The current month is September, so this dashboard is using the Autumn seasonal grouping.

  • Anomaly detection: each selected Forward Marks + Balmo mark is checked against its own history and against a trust-gated peer view built from correlated subgroup or parent-group markets.
  • Historical check: this is explanation-only and does not change the flag. Forward compares the transition from the current month actual-to-date basis into the next-month forward mark, for example July mark minus June actual-to-date median. Balmo compares the transition from the latest actual basis into the balance-of-month mark, for example June 20-30 Balmo mark minus June 19 actual basis. Each transition is compared with the same calendar setup in prior years. For weaker/stronger transitions, degree labels (slight, mild, moderate, strong, very strong) scale the magnitude gap by each component's own current-month daily basis standard deviation, falling back to that component's historical same-month standard deviation when current-month data is sparse.
  • Strong outlier: a high-conviction dislocation. The market is stretched versus normal history and/or clearly out of line with trusted peers.
  • Outlier: a real dislocation that is large enough to investigate and discuss, even if it is not the most extreme print on the page.
  • Watch: an early warning signal. The market is starting to move away from its usual range or peer relationships, but the signal is not yet strong enough to call a full outlier.
